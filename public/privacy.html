Privacy

Local-first · no account required

What stays on your device

  • Restaurant choice, store pick, theme, and install-banner dismiss state
  • Downloaded menu snapshots and last sync time (browser storage)
  • Basket / wishlist work for the session

What leaves your device

  • Store search and menu requests go through our proxy to restaurant systems for prices
  • We do not create an account or sync your basket to a ComboWise server
  • Optional Google Tag Manager / analytics only if VITE_GTM_ID is set at build time; otherwise no GTM/GA script is loaded. Control tracking via browser settings as well.

Your controls

Use Clear in the restaurant toolbar to wipe that chain’s local data. Clear site data in the browser to remove everything for this origin.
